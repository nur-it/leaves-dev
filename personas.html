I've been feeling unsure about my next career step. Everything feels blurry right now.

It's completely normal to feel uncertain. Life doesn't always give us clear signals. What matters is paying attention to what energizes you and what drains you. Those feelings usually point you in the right direction.

I guess I'm just afraid of making the wrong choice again.

There's rarely a "wrong" choice — only choices that teach you something. Think about it: every step you've taken, even the tough ones, shaped who you are today.

That helps... but I also feel pressure to make the perfect decision. Like time's running out.

Time isn't running out.
